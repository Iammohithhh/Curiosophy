Following curiosity, wherever it leads.

Curiosophy is my open notebook: things I'm trying to understand, papers I'm working through, and problems that give me a reason to learn something difficult.

Right now, that problem is quantum chemistry. Can we predict what molecules will do accurately enough to be useful, without making every calculation prohibitively expensive?

**[[challenge/index|Start the investigation: Can we make quantum chemistry both fast and accurate?]]**

The story starts with the problem and follows the ideas behind density functional theory, physical constraints, hybrid functionals and machine learning. Equations are unpacked along the way. You can follow a tangent and come back.

- [[papers/index|Papers]] — close readings of the ideas that changed the problem.
- [[learnings/index|Learnings]] — the mathematical and scientific connections behind them.

This is a learning project, not a claim to have solved the challenge. The questions are part of the notebook.
