Two identical, far-separated fragments share one extra electron. Let q go to fragment A and 1 − q to B.

━ Exact ensemble reference━ Curved approximation

Each fragment contributes a bias −c q(1 − q), so together the error is −2c q(1 − q). Energies are arbitrary units relative to the straight-line reference. At infinite separation, identical fragments have degenerate exact ensemble partitions; the curvature adds an artificial preference.
